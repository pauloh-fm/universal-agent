# Returns and warranty

Unused items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Return shipping is free for defective or wrong items.

Refunds go to the original payment method within 5 business days after the return arrives at the warehouse.

All products have a 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects. Damage from misuse is not covered.

To start a return or a warranty claim, the assistant opens a ticket and the team replies by e-mail within 1 business day.
