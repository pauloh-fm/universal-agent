# Shipping

Orders are processed within 1 business day. Standard delivery takes 3 to 7 business days after dispatch.

Shipping costs 9.90 USD per order and is free for orders of 150 USD or more.

A tracking code is sent by e-mail when the order ships. Customers can also ask the assistant for the order status with the order id and e-mail.

Orders can be cancelled free of charge while their status is "placed" or "processing". After shipping, use the returns process.
