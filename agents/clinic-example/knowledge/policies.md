# Policies

Please arrive 10 minutes before your appointment to complete check-in.

Cancellations and rescheduling are free up to 24 hours before the appointment. Late cancellations or no-shows may be charged 50% of the service price.

If you are more than 15 minutes late, the appointment may need to be rescheduled.

Payment is accepted by card or cash at the front desk after the appointment. Receipts are issued for insurance reimbursement.

We accept the fictitious plans "Example Health Plus" and "Sample Care". For other insurance, pay at the visit and request reimbursement from your insurer.

Minors under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian.
