# About the clinic

Example Health Clinic is a fictitious outpatient clinic used to demonstrate the template. Address: 100 Example Street, Suite 200, Sampletown.

Front desk phone: +1 555 0100 (fictitious). Front desk hours are the same as the clinic opening hours.

Parking is available in the building garage; the first hour is free for patients.
